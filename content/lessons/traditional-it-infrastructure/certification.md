## Certification relevance

### AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02)
**Domain 1: Cloud Concepts.**
- *Task 1.1, benefits of the AWS Cloud*: every benefit is defined relative to on-premises. You must recognise the **six advantages of cloud computing** in scenario form.
- *Task 1.4, cloud economics*: **fixed vs. variable cost**, costs associated with on-premises environments, licensing strategies, and the concept of **right-sizing**.

### What the exam expects you to recognise
| If the question says… | Think… |
|---|---|
| "large up-front investment", "capital expense" | On-prem (CapEx). The cloud trades it for variable expense. |
| "idle capacity", "over-provisioned" | Stop guessing capacity / economies of scale |
| "weeks to procure hardware" | Increase speed and agility |
| "racking, stacking and powering servers" | Stop spending money running and maintaining data centres |
| "costs of power, cooling, space, staff" | Total cost of ownership (TCO) |
| "reduce time to market" | Agility |

### Typical question styles
- *"Which is a benefit of moving from on-premises to the AWS Cloud?"* Look for the option that matches one of the six advantages. Distractors often say things like "AWS manages your application code" (false) or "no security responsibility" (false).
- *"Which costs are part of on-premises TCO but not usually of the AWS Cloud?"* Hardware purchase, data-centre space, power and cooling, physical security.
- *"A company wants to avoid large up-front costs…"* The answer is the pay-as-you-go/variable-expense model.

### Exam traps
- **"The cloud is always cheaper."** The exam never says this. It says you can **lower** total costs through economies of scale, pay-as-you-go and right-sizing.
- **CapEx vs. OpEx direction.** On-prem = mostly CapEx. Cloud = mostly OpEx. Don't flip them under time pressure.
- **"Undifferentiated heavy lifting"** refers to running infrastructure, not to writing your application.

### Later certifications
**Solutions Architect – Associate (SAA-C03)** assumes all of this, and adds migration scenarios: hybrid connectivity, phased migration, and keeping some systems on-prem while others move.
